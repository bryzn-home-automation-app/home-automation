Phone alerts

Which alerts buzz your phone is up to you, under Alert types. Camera alerts and Roomba errors are on to start with.

Not getting alerts?Hide the help
  • Opened in Safari, not the Home Screen app 1. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. 2. Open HomeOS from the new icon. 3. Come back to Profile and turn alerts on.
  • Notifications are blocked Allow them in Settings → Notifications → HomeOS, then come back here.

## Notes from the shell (phase 1c)

- The tab now sits in the new shell: the shell draws its title ("House" / "Maintenance") above the page, so its own hero card repeats the title until the tab is converted (drop the hero then).
- Its Add / Edit / Detail overlays are not marked as dialogs (`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`) and do not use `.modal-overlay`, so pull-to-refresh can still fire behind them (B-043). Building them on the kit dialog (`fp-dialog`, see IMPLEMENTATION.md 3.5) fixes that too.
- Guests are still bounced from `/maintenance` to Home by `MemberRoute`; the menus already hide it from them.

## Notes from the integration pass (phase 1d)

- The tab renders inside the new frame and is usable in both themes (screenshots: phone and desktop, light and dark).
- In the Add / Edit dialog on desktop, the priority keys (Low / Medium / High / Emergency) are wider than their half
  of the row, so "Emergency" runs under the Status select. The kit's `Chips` would wrap them.
- The fonts are self-hosted now (no Google Fonts); nothing for this tab to change.

## Notes from the final pass (phase 2)

- Still renders and works inside the converted app in both themes (phone and desktop shots in the final harness run).
- The record details dialog on a phone: the three date tiles are so narrow that "SCHEDULED" and "COMPLETED" are cut
  off at the tile's edge and the dates wrap to three lines (B-142's details half, now plain to see).
- The Add dialog on a phone has the same Priority / Status collision as on desktop: "HIGH" runs under the Status select.
- `index.css` keeps the light-only pastel remap (`:root:not([data-theme="dark"])`) only for this tab's palette
  classes; delete it when the tab is converted (`indexCss.test.ts` checks it is there, so update that test too).
- Its overlays are still `fixed inset-0` divs without `role="dialog"`, and the `.modal-overlay` utility is gone, so
  PullToRefresh does not see them as open overlays (B-043); building them on the kit `Dialog` fixes it.

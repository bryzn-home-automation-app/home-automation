Version 1.10.0 · Sat, Nov 7

Gas, by the bill

The Gas tab now shows what each gas bill used and paid for, and when the next one comes.

Fixed2 changes

  • On an iPhone, live view no longer sticks on “Waking camera…” when the video has arrived. If the phone holds it back, one tap on Play starts every camera.
  • The Living Room camera no longer takes half a minute to start when someone's in the room.

New2 changes

  • Charts of gas used and gas charges for every bill, with the outside temperature for each billing cycle.
  • The last gas bill is broken down line by line, with what a CCF of gas really costs and what you pay with none.

Improved1 change

  • Gas shows your last bill, the gas it used, its flat charges and when the next bill should arrive.

Fixed1 change

  • Gas costs used a made-up price per unit and missed the flat charges, which are about half the bill.

Fixed1 change

  • On an iPhone, the bottom bar no longer floats up the screen as you scroll. Pages now scroll above it instead of under it.

Improved1 change

  • When Server health stops hearing from the home server, it says the numbers are from the last report and how to get the monitor going again.

Fixed1 change

  • On an iPhone, the bottom bar no longer drifts up the screen with the page after the menu or a pop-up has been open.

New4 changes

  • Admin → Server health shows the home server's processor, memory, drive, temperatures and Wi-Fi now, and over the last day, week or month.
  • It lists each part of the app with the memory it uses, and the programs working the home server hardest.
  • Problems are spelled out: a drive filling up, the app itself not responding, running hot, set to sleep, or Windows waiting to restart.
  • Admins are told when the home server restarts, and whether it crashed or lost power, and when a problem lasts.

Fixed1 change

  • Water bills come in again: the Gmail sign-in no longer runs out every week.

New1 change

  • The admin Activity log lists every thermostat change someone made: at the thermostat, in the ecobee app, or in this app.

Improved1 change

  • A thermostat card says “Set by schedule” when ecobee's schedule changed it, instead of “Set at ecobee”.

Fixed6 changes

  • On a phone, a recording now has a close button you can reach, and the back gesture closes it instead of leaving Cameras. The same goes for a bill.
  • Edit profile and a Roomba run's details fit on a phone screen, so Save and Close can be reached.
  • Swiping on a pop-up no longer scrolls the page behind it.
  • Notifications no longer overlap each other on a phone.
  • Pages start below the status bar in the Home Screen app.
  • A change made in this app was recorded twice.

New5 changes

  • Weather alerts under Profile: rain, thunderstorms, freeze, extreme heat, high wind, and snow or ice.
  • Each is checked at a time you pick, for the hours you pick. Rain at 8 PM through 8 AM tells you whether to pause the sprinklers.
  • Set what counts: the chance of rain, how cold, how hot, or how windy.
  • Official National Weather Service warnings for home (tornado, severe storm, flood and more) as soon as they're issued.
  • “Check the forecast now” shows what an alert would say right now.

Fixed1 change

  • The phone menu scrolls on its own instead of scrolling the page behind it.

New3 changes

  • Roomba runs show the rooms cleaned, the settings, recharge stops and how often the bin was emptied.
  • Tap Full screen on a live camera to watch it bigger.
  • An alert when a Roomba clean doesn't finish (stuck), with what stopped it.

Improved7 changes

  • Roomba history comes from iRobot's own records: real cleaning time and area, and runs that happened while the server was off.
  • The Roomba tab says when it's docked mid-clean to recharge instead of calling the run finished.
  • The Roomba's live position no longer drops out every hour during a clean.
  • The daily usage report waits until CoServ has posted the whole day.
  • The monthly estimate covers all of last month, not the first day or two of this one.
  • Admins get an alert when the water-bill Gmail sign-in expires.
  • After an update the app reloads itself instead of showing a blank page; a broken page no longer takes down the whole app.

Fixed5 changes

  • Pages that need sign-in can no longer be reached without it.
  • Maintenance dates no longer show one day early, and editing a record no longer duplicates its photos.
  • A thermostat change made just before leaving the page is still sent.
  • “Last updated” times show correctly on iPhone and on the Diagnostics page.
  • Signing out clears what the last person was looking at; a hiccup while the server restarts no longer signs you out.

New11 changes

  • Phone alerts for camera and critical alerts from the Home Screen app (Profile → Phone alerts).
  • Alert types: choose which alerts you get, and which reach your phone (Profile → Alert types).
  • Recordings: play back the cameras' full-quality 3K clips from the HomeBase (Cameras → Recordings).
  • Send cameras to their saved positions, and pan or tilt them during live view.
  • Profile → This device: link the phone you're holding to your account in two taps, so the app knows when you're home.
  • Indoor camera alerts while everyone's away: someone seen inside, or a few minutes of noise (the noise alert needs the camera's Sound detection on).
  • On your phone, pull down at the top of any page to refresh it.
  • Auto theme: light by day, dark after sunset at home (Profile → Theme → Auto).
  • Sleep screen: left open and untouched for 10 minutes, the app sleeps and the cameras pause (a minute's warning first; tap to wake).
  • Who's home (Admin): see who's home from the phones on the home Wi-Fi. Name each device, pick its type and its owner.
  • App usage (Admin): see which tabs house members use the most, updated every minute.

Improved5 changes

  • The Cameras tab opens already live, outdoor cameras too: they warm up while you use the app.
  • Live video pauses after 5 idle minutes.
  • Phone menu bar: the thermostats and Cameras are one tap away; Alerts moved into Menu, which shows the unread count.
  • The electric forecast misses by about a third less: it learns from the weather that actually happened, and from weekends.
  • Colors that were hard to read in light or dark mode are fixed.

Fixed3 changes

  • Signing in on iPhone with a saved password works again.
  • Your color palette stays put in light and dark mode, and after signing back in.
  • On the Default palette, Predicted and Actual on the forecast chart are no longer both green.

New5 changes

  • Climate tab: see and set both ecobee thermostats, with history and who changed them last.
  • A heads-up before you override someone's thermostat change from the last 10 minutes.
  • Cameras tab: latest snapshots, plus live video that only runs while you're watching.
  • Battery and solar-charging history for the outdoor cameras.
  • Camera alerts for low battery, no solar charging, offline cameras and people outside.

Improved2 changes

  • Pages load about twice as fast, especially on phones.
  • Bills now open only for signed-in members, and disabled accounts lose access right away.

Fixed1 change

  • A day's electric total that first arrives as zero now corrects itself.

New6 changes

  • Electric, gas and water bills open right in the app.
  • The Electric tab is split into Usage & bills and Forecast & stats.
  • The last 30 days next to the next 14 predicted, plus predicted against actual for the last reading.
  • Day-by-day forecast accuracy.
  • Roomba Maintenance: how much life is left on the filter, brushes, mop pad and dock bag.
  • A pixel-art scene on every tab.

Improved3 changes

  • The forecast learns from yesterday's miss and is honest about far-out days.
  • Alerts moved to a bell icon, and long lists scroll smoothly.
  • The Guest Wi-Fi page is a single QR code.

Fixed2 changes

  • Gas charts no longer come up blank or show highs and lows of 0.
  • Sign-in ignores capital letters in your username.

Improved4 changes

  • The forecast updates within minutes of a new reading.
  • One unusual day no longer throws off the trend.
  • The shaded range adapts faster to how accurate recent predictions were.
  • Section titles are larger and easier to read.

Fixed2 changes

  • The shaded range is easier to see and no longer vanishes for yesterday.
  • Water bill amounts show a full-size $ sign.

New2 changes

  • See which weekdays you use more or less power than the weather predicts.
  • Roomba schedules save to the robot and appear in the iRobot app.

Improved3 changes

  • The 7- and 14-day forecast views show all their days.
  • Yesterday's usage shows up a day sooner.
  • A narrower, easier-to-read forecast range.

Fixed2 changes

  • More reliable Roomba commands.
  • Corrected daily trends, rolling averages and partial-day totals.

New1 change

  • Clean: choose rooms, suction, passes and vacuum, mop or both.

Improved1 change

  • The robot still docks itself when it's done.

Fixed2 changes

  • Editing your profile no longer lags.
  • Other pop-ups respond more smoothly too.

Improved5 changes

  • Screens and charts respond faster.
  • Home greets you by name and loads lighter.
  • The live vacuum map stays smooth while it cleans.
  • Theme and accent color changes are instant.
  • Smoother scrolling on phones and in long lists.

Fixed1 change

  • Usage charts no longer fail to appear when a page opens.

New8 changes

  • Electricity tracking, compared against the weather.
  • A running estimate of this month's electric bill.
  • Gas and water tabs.
  • The robot vacuum's live map, cleaning history and room cleaning.
  • A guest Wi-Fi QR code.
  • Alerts for unusual usage.
  • Personal accounts with a photo and theme, plus limited guest access.
  • This What's new page.
